At Hazel Family Law & Mediation, we know that no two families, and no two people, are the same. Every client brings their own experiences, strengths and challenges. For some, the family law process itself can feel overwhelming, especially when managing mental health conditions, neurodivergence such as autism or ADHD or sensory sensitivities.



We believe the legal process should work *for* you, not against you. That is why we focus on meeting you where you are at and, when possible, adapting our approach, communication and environment to support your individual needs and help you feel safe, respected and heard.



# **Understanding comes first**



Family law issues often arise at a time of emotional strain. For clients managing ADHD, autism or other mental health or neurological conditions, traditional legal processes can unintentionally create barriers. We take the time to understand not just your legal situation, but how you best communicate and process information.



That may mean allowing extra time in meetings, using plain language instead of legal jargon or providing information in writing so you can revisit it later. Sometimes it means slowing things down, breaking large decisions into manageable steps or offering shorter, more frequent check-ins instead of long appointments.



# **Creating a calmer legal experience**



We aim to create a calm and supportive environment, one that recognises how stress, overwhelm or sensory sensitivities can affect your ability to participate fully. Practical examples of how we can do this include:



* **Flexible meeting formats:** offering phone, video or in-person sessions and choosing quieter times of day for clients who find busy environments difficult.
* **Clear, consistent communication:** using visual summaries or step-by-step outlines so you always know what is happening next.
* **Pacing the process:** breaking complex discussions into smaller, scheduled sessions so you have time to process information between meetings.
* **Supportive collaboration:** where appropriate, we can work alongside your therapist, support worker or advocate to help ensure the process aligns with your wellbeing needs.
* **Environment adjustments:** soft lighting, reduced background noise or familiar sensory aids during appointments when needed.



Our goal is always the same, to help you engage in the process with clarity and confidence, in a way that feels safe and manageable for you.



# **Communication that works for you**



Everyone absorbs and processes information differently. For some, it is easier to have things explained visually or in writing. For others, face-to-face conversations are clearer. We will work with you to find what works best and we will check in regularly to make sure it is still working.



We also understand that decision-making can be harder during times of stress or when managing executive functioning challenges. We can assist by prioritising issues, setting timelines together and ensuring key decisions are not rushed.



# **Compassion and professionalism can co-exist**



Flexibility does not mean lowering professional standards, it means meeting those standards in a way that is accessible and respectful. By recognising that clients come to us with different capacities, communication styles and coping mechanisms, we are able to provide legal support that truly aligns with our values of clarity, compassion and resolution.



# **A shared commitment**



If you are navigating family separation, parenting arrangements or property settlement and feel worried about managing the process, please know you are not alone. Tell us what you need. We will listen without judgment and work with you to adapt our process wherever possible.



At Hazel Family Law & Mediation, we believe that fairness in family law begins with accessibility. Meeting you where you are is not just about kindness; it is about ensuring every person has a real and equal opportunity to be heard and to achieve resolution.
